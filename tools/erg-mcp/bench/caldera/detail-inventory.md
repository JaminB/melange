# Caldera detail layer: the art inventory

Generated by `scenery_catalog {includeSim: true, limit: 2000}` over every game base (69 bases, 364 resources of the
visual channels) on 2026-11-09, testenv D. Sizes are each piece's preview mesh (the Erg panel's GLB) at the example's
own scale, map units (1 map unit = 20 world; a worm is about 0.55 wide and 0.75 tall). `example id` is the detail to
pass to `copy_base_details {base, copies: [{id, pos, rot, scale}]}` (or `copy_base_scenery` for meshes). A piece of
another theme than the level's (PREHISTORIC) needs `allowOtherTheme`. The round-0 contact sheet (`caldet_r0_contact_sheet.png`
in the round-0 evidence, 32 pieces from 9 themes placed by `detail-probe.mjs`) shows that other themes' pieces load and draw
in a PREHISTORIC level, but the copies did not stand where the editor put them, so a tile's label is not reliably the
piece in it (docs/erg-mcp.md, "Detail layer", limits): check placement in a Test before choosing by the sheet.

**What there is, and is not.** The bases' scenery details are small props: grass tufts, flowers, a few rocks (0.7-2.2
units), dinosaur eggs, skulls, cacti (2-3 units), mushrooms. There are **no trees, palms, ferns, vines, dead trees,
large boulders, cliffs, crystals or basalt pieces** among any base's details: the game's big rocks and trees are the
levels' voxel frames or their landscape meshes, which are not details (and voxels are sim). Big rocks for the volcano
are therefore scaled copies (a copy's scale is free, 0.05-20: ARABIAN6 at 3x is a 6.6-unit boulder) or voxels (sim:
not for this layer). Many of the bases' grass tufts and the TyrannoSawUs rocks are named `VISIBLE_MORTAL_` /
`VISABLE_MORTAL_`: explosions remove them, a sim effect, so they are sim and not copied (copy the non-MORTAL rows below).
